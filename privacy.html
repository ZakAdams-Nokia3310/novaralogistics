Novara Terra Industrials (Pty) Ltd — Last updated: 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Novara Terra Industrials (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use the EquipCore platform. We are committed to complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

databaseWhat Data We Collect

  • Account data: Name, email address, role, and organisation.
  • Application data: ID number, date of birth, phone number, address, employment details, financial information submitted in rental applications.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, actions taken, timestamps — recorded in audit logs for security and compliance.
  • Technical data: Browser type, operating system, approximate location (country) — used to detect fraud and improve security.
  • Cookies: Essential session cookies only. No tracking or advertising cookies are set without your explicit consent.

task_altWhy We Collect It

  • To authenticate users and manage access to the platform.
  • To process rental applications and perform credit assessments.
  • To maintain security audit trails as required by law.
  • To send transactional notifications (rental confirmations, maintenance alerts).
  • To comply with applicable South African financial and transport regulations.

We apply data minimisation — we only collect information that is strictly necessary for the above purposes.

shareWho We Share Data With

  • Firebase (Google): Hosting, authentication, and database infrastructure. Data is stored in Google Cloud under their Data Processing Addendum.
  • Credit bureaux: ID number and financial information is shared with licensed credit bureaux only for rental application processing, with your explicit consent.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

lockHow We Protect Your Data

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Sensitive fields (ID numbers, financial data) are masked in the user interface.
  • Access to personal data is restricted by role — only admins can view all records.
  • Audit logs record every access, change, and deletion.
  • Firebase Security Rules enforce per-resource access control.
  • Session tokens expire after 8 hours of inactivity.

scheduleData Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active and for up to 7 years after account closure to comply with South African financial record-keeping requirements. Audit logs are retained for 2 years. Rental applications are retained for 5 years.

verified_userYour Rights (POPIA / GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Delete your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Port your data — receive a copy in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): justice.gov.za/inforeg

Exercise Your Rights

Data export downloads your locally stored data. For server-side records, email privacy@equipcore.co.za. We will respond within 30 days as required by POPIA.

cookieCookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication and security. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are set without your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and local storage.
